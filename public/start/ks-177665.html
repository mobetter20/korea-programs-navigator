University students, general public, universities, research institutions, general companies, solo entrepreneurs. As of announcement date (May 12, 2026), applicants meeting all of the following 1-3 are eligible: 1. Seoul resident or living in Seoul metropolitan area: local residents, employees in Seoul, Seoul university/graduate students in attendance or leave of absence. (Note: Individual or team applications accepted; team applications require minimum 1 person in Seoul living area.) 2. Prospective entrepreneurs and companies within 7 years of founding. 1) Prospective entrepreneurs. 2) General business operators: business registration date May 13, 2019 or later. 3) Corporate business operators: company establishment date May 13, 2019 or later. 4) Exception: new industry startup field companies may apply up to 10 years of operation. (Note: New industry startup field: 27 designated fields per Small and Medium Enterprise Startup Support Act Article 25(4), see official announcement.) 3. Representative aged 39 or younger: born May 13, 1986 or later (including May 13, 1986 birth). (Note: Exceptions: 1) Discharged military personnel may receive up to 3 additional years under Seoul Youth Basic Ordinance. 2) In-campus startups and international student founders are exempt from age restrictions.)

Who's excluded

1. Currently receiving support from other university startup spaces in Seoul Campustown program. 2. Companies engaged in startup-excluded industries under Small and Medium Enterprise Startup Support Act Enforcement Decree Article 4. (1) General entertainment bars, (2) dance entertainment bars, (3) casino operations, (4) other gambling facility management and operation businesses, (5) other industries contrary to economic order and public morals specified by SME and Startup Ministry. 3. Individuals or companies currently regulated for financial institution debt defaults, national tax delinquency, or local tax delinquency. 4. Companies in suspension as of announcement date (May 12, 2026). 5. Companies relocated from Seoul startup support facilities due to issues. 6. Other cases deemed to have participation restriction reasons by Hanyang University Campustown program.
