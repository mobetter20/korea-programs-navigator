Who's excluded

1. Companies currently participating in startup incubation or similar workspace support programs from other institutions (government, local governments, public institutions, universities, etc.). 2. Companies that benefited from workspace support programs of other institutions in 2026 (short-term and long-term included). 3. Companies with prior history of receiving workspace benefits from Daejeon-Sejong Tourism Business Support Center. (Note: Existing beneficiaries may apply for short-term residency, but companies that benefited in the current year and companies that received 3 or more cumulative short-term residency support are excluded). 4. Companies subject to credit information registration for financial institution debt defaults. 5. Companies with unpaid national or local taxes. 6. Companies subject to participation restrictions from government and public institution programs. 7. Companies in suspension or discontinuation as of application date. 8. Companies engaged in startup-excluded industries under the "Startup Support Law for Small and Medium Enterprises" (general entertainment bars, dance entertainment bars, gambling facility management and operation businesses, etc.). 9. Other companies deemed necessary for participation restriction by the administering agency based on program objectives and operating standards.
