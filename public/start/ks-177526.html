CTS program (2026–2027) funds Korean innovators to apply their tech solving real problems in developing nations. Open to social-impact founders.

Cash or in-kind support to commercialize and grow your business.

Who can apply

University students, general public, general companies, solo entrepreneurs: common requirement - prospective entrepreneurs, individual proprietors within 10 years of business, or companies under the Commercial Act within 10 years of business; track structure: SEED 1 (ODA testbed, max 400 million won support) - refine change theory, beneficiaries, stakeholders of development cooperation innovation solutions and validate local applicability and initial commercialization through PoC, prototype, small-scale demonstration; SEED 2 (max 700 million won support) - distribute and expand development cooperation solutions locally, demonstrate business structure, verify social impact and business sustainability through beneficiary expansion and independent operating systems; CTS-TIPS linked (max 1.1 billion won support) - integrate SEED 1 and 2 for accelerated local demonstration to commercialization, simultaneously verify social impact and business sustainability in short timeframe, companies receiving cumulative 2 billion won or greater private investment as of announcement date; companies meeting above conditions and any 1 of 3 conditions: SMBA TIPS successful companies, KOICA CTS SEED 1/2 graduate companies, CES Consumer Electronics Show Innovation Award-winning companies.
