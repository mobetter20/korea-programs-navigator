Startup-BuS Program: get direct investment, TIPS funding matched, and demo day pitch coaching. Designed to speed your path to unicorn growth.

Cash or in-kind support to commercialize and grow your business.

Who can apply
<7yr

Youth, university students, general public, general companies, solo entrepreneurs: startups within 7 years (no geographic restrictions on company location; founding companies per Small and Medium Enterprise Startup Support Act Article 2-3 within 7 years of founding as of announcement date).

This program is for already-operating businesses — verify registration requirements on the official posting.

Eligibility translated from the official Korean posting — confirm there before applying.

Who's excluded

As of application date, individuals/companies on hiatus or closed; individuals/companies in default by financial institutions (exceptions: individuals/companies able to prove payment completion by application deadline, those with debt restructuring agreements under credit recovery committee pre-workout or personal workout, those approved for repayment plans under court personal debt adjustment or discharged from bankruptcy, companies approved for business rehabilitation, individuals/companies recognized as needing government/public institution restart support from organizations like Small and Venture Enterprise Promotion Agency); individuals/companies with national or local tax arrears (exceptions: companies with successful payment plans following tax withholding procedures, individuals/companies able to prove payment completion of national/local special debts by deadline); individuals/companies on wage arrears lists published by the Ministry of Employment and Labor; individuals/companies excluded from startup under Small and Medium Enterprise Startup Support Act Enforcement Decree Article 4 or deemed to have participation restriction reasons by the Minister of SMEs and Startups.
