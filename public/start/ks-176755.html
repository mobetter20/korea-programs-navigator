Who can apply
Pre-launch<1yr<2yr<3yr<5yr<7yr

Youth, university students, general public, universities, research institutions, general enterprises, solo entrepreneurs. Prospective entrepreneurs (teams) or startups within 7 years. No geographic restrictions. Prospective entrepreneurs: those without business registration or corporate registration as of announcement date (individuals/teams). Startups: companies founded within 7 years per the Small and Medium Enterprise Establishment Support Act, Article 2, Section 3, as of announcement date.

Eligibility translated from the official Korean posting — confirm there before applying.

Who's excluded

Individuals or companies currently suspended or closed as of application date. Individuals or companies in debt default from financial institutions (exceptions: those who complete debt repayment by application deadline with proof; those with debt adjustment agreements from the Credit Recovery Committee's pre-workout or individual workout; those with personal rehabilitation repayment plans approved by courts or discharged bankrupts; companies receiving rehabilitation approval; those receiving comeback funds from SMBA, INNOBIZ, Credit Guarantee Fund, Korea Technology Finance Corporation, K-Startup Foundation, or other government/public institutions recognized as needing restructuring support are eligible to apply). Individuals or companies with overdue national or local taxes (exceptions: companies making faithful payments per tax installment plans, or those with proof of payment completion by application deadline are eligible). Individuals or companies on the Ministry of Employment and Labor's wage arrears list. Individuals or companies excluded under Article 4 of the Small and Medium Enterprise Establishment Support Act Enforcement Decree or those the Small and Medium Business Administration Minister deems to have participation restriction reasons.
