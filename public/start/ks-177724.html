Who's excluded

Companies in simple trade or distribution, or unrelated to the exhibition items; companies whose main office or factory is not located in Seongnam from the application date through the participation period; companies in arrears on national or local taxes (including deferred collection); companies whose NICE credit check is unavailable or returns doubtful-recovery, closure/suspension, or insolvency; companies under court receivership, corporate rehabilitation, or liquidation; companies receiving support from another agency or local government for the same exhibition.
