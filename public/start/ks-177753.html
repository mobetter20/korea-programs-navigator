KU Global Startlink finds early-stage founders with innovative tech or ideas and coaches them for international scale.

Subsidized or free workspace and incubation.

Who can apply
Pre-launch<1yr<2yr<3yr<5yr<7yr

Teenagers, university students, general public, universities, research institutions, general companies, solo entrepreneurs. Eligibility: Individuals or teams with at least 1 foreign member. Representative age 39 or under, intending to start a business (prospective entrepreneurs). Representative age 39 or under, business initiated within 7 years, individual or corporate business operator. For new-industry startups under Startup Support Act § 25(4), up to 10 years. Exception: Korea University (고려대학교) on-campus entrepreneurs (faculty, students, alumni) and foreign student entrepreneurs exempt from age restrictions. Age and business tenure standards apply at recruitment announcement date. For veterans: per Seoul Youth Basic Ordinance, age limit raised as follows: 2+ years service: age 42 or under; 1-2 years service: age 41 or under; under 1 year service: age 40 or under. For co-representatives, eligibility satisfied if any one meets criteria. Relocation to Korea University Campus Town startup support space within 3 months of lease signing possible. For early entrepreneurs: register/transfer business address within 3 months; prospective entrepreneurs within 6 months; corporate headquarters, branches, or corporate research institutes may be established through consultation.

Eligibility translated from the official Korean posting — confirm there before applying.

Who's excluded

Startup enterprises falling under §4 clauses of the Startup Support Act Implementation Ordinance. Entities regulated by financial institutions for loan default or national/local tax delinquency. Exceptions: pre-arranged debt restructuring (pre-workout) or personal workout agreement, court individual rehabilitation plan approval, bankruptcy discharge, or enterprise rehabilitation approval, or small-business promotion agency restarting funds, or tax installment plan compliance, or full tax payment before government contract, or government public agency recognition of restarting need, or enterprise structure improvement diagnosis approval. Entities receiving Seoul Campus Town project support at other university startup spaces. Exceptions: those with project support period ending before move-in or abandoning project. Enterprises on hiatus as of application date. Entities required to vacate due to Seoul Campus Town project issues. Other entities deemed by Seoul Mayor to have participation restriction cause.
