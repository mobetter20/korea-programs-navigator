Run a train station store: Korea Railroad outlet

[코레일유통/본사] 제3차 청년창업 제휴사업자 모집공고

Korea Railroad Distribution recruits young entrepreneurs to operate cafes and food shops in train stations. Three locations open.

Subsidized or free workspace and incubation.

Who can apply
Pre-launch<3yr

University students, general public. Age 18+ and 39 or under as of announcement date. As of announcement date, no business registration or established within 3 years per Small and Medium Enterprise Basic Law § 2 clause 2 (representative age condition required). Capable of full general tax registration within 7 days of contract and facility investment. Unaffiliated franchise operator with proprietary brand and recipe operation capability.

Eligibility translated from the official Korean posting — confirm there before applying.

Who's excluded

Special relationship with company or public interest restrictions as follows: (1) Selected as Korail Distribution youth startup support store operator and spouse/direct relatives. (2) Korail Distribution designated unfair operator and spouse/direct relatives. (3) Existing contractors (as of announcement): (1) In or with prior history of Ureisa convenience stores/vending machines and spouse/direct relatives; (2) Operating Ureisa specialized stores or with prior history and spouse/direct relatives. (4) Company special relationships (as of announcement): (1) Direct relatives of former employees with less than 2 years elapsed since retirement as of recruitment start. (2) Direct relatives of current employees of Korean Railroad Corporation/subsidiaries. (5) Persons with multiple retail facilities as of announcement seeking expansion and spouse/direct relatives.
